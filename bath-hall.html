Bath notes on the sheet

  • Shower valve is pressure-balancing or thermostatic.
  • Shower tile runs 72" up over backer board.
  • Shower door at least 22" wide, and the floor must fit a 30" circle.
  • Blocking in the walls for towel bars and the medicine cabinet.
  • Fan is 80 CFM on a humidistat and doubles as the whole-house ventilation fan, so it runs continuously.
  • Lights on a vacancy sensor.
  • One GFI outlet on its own 20-amp circuit.
  • Vanity, cabinets and flooring are left to the owner.

Works well for aging parents

  • Short trips at night. Both bedroom doors open onto the hall, and the bathroom door is no more than about 10 feet from either.
  • The shower footprint is big enough. At about 3 by 5 feet it is the size of a roll-in shower. What matters is how it is built, covered in the next column.
  • Room for a grab bar at the toilet. The toilet sits about 18" off the shower wall, the spacing a side grab bar needs.
  • The kitchen route has no doors. From the hall it is one cased opening, then an open nook where a 5-foot turning circle fits easily.
  • Laundry is steps from both bedrooms. No carrying baskets through the house.

Raise with the architect

  • Hall is 3 feet wide with 2'-8" doors. A wheelchair turning from a 36" hall needs a wider door than the 30" clear these give. Widen the hall to 42" or more, or make the bath and bedroom doors 3'-0".
  • Bath door swings in. If someone falls against it, help cannot get in. A 3'-0" pocket or sliding door fixes that and frees floor space.
  • No turning space inside the bathroom. About 3'-6" is clear between the vanity and the hall wall, so a 5-foot circle does not fit. A wall-hung vanity with open knee space, or a shallower one, helps most.
  • Build the shower curbless. A hot-mop pan normally means a curb. Ask for a flush entry along the long side, a curtain or wide opening instead of a 22" door, a fold-down seat and a hand shower.
  • Blocking for grab bars. The sheet only calls for towel-bar blocking. Add plywood backing around the toilet and all three shower walls now, while the studs are open.
  • Toilet to vanity. The gap scales close to the 15" code minimum from the toilet centerline. Confirm it on the full-size sheet.
  • Stacked washer and dryer. The dryer controls and door end up high. Side-by-side front loaders need about 5 feet of width, which this closet does not have.
