Kitchen notes on the sheet

  • Range hood with light and fan, vented to the outside.
  • At least two 20-amp circuits for kitchen appliances.
  • Every countertop outlet is GFI protected.
  • Under-cabinet lights, switched separately from the ceiling lights.
  • Faucet limited to 1.8 gallons per minute.
  • Dishwasher drain needs an air gap fitting.
  • Countertops, cabinets, flooring and appliances are left to the owner.

Works well for aging parents

  • The aisle is generous. About 7'-9" between the counter legs leaves room for a 5-foot wheelchair turning circle with space to spare.
  • Sink to range is one continuous counter. A heavy pot can slide around the corner instead of being carried.
  • Dishwasher sits beside the sink. No wet dishes cross the aisle.
  • Good light on the main work run. Two windows and three recessed lights sit over the sink and dishwasher.

Raise with the architect

  • Deck door is 2'-8". That leaves about 30" clear. A 3'-0" door with a low threshold suits a walker or wheelchair, and the swing lands at the end of the range counter.
  • No set-down space beside the refrigerator. It sits between a wall and the pantry. Swapping the pantry and the counter section puts a landing spot next to it.
  • Two blind corner cabinets. Specify corner pull-outs or drawers so nothing needs a deep reach on bent knees.
  • Range type. An induction range with front controls avoids reaching over hot burners and cools quickly.
  • A seated work spot. The wide aisle allows one section of counter at 30" to 34" high with open knee space below.
